Plans

Plans for one muscle group, and plans that combine several. Each card lists the stations in the order you use them, so you only move a few times. Warm up with one or two light sets, rest about 60 seconds between sets, and pick a weight where the last two reps are hard but clean. The manual suggests 60–80% of your max for 8–12 reps to build muscle, or 40–50% for 15–20 reps for endurance, and at least 10 kg on the pulley station so the cable stays on its pulleys.

A full body, across the week

Six sessions of about half an hour, built from the plans for several muscle groups. Together they train every muscle group at least once, and most get two sessions. Swap any plan for one that trains the same muscles if you want variety.

The reference model

A scale model of the Unit 6 in centimetres, measured from the manufacturer’s side and front photos and checked against the parts list. Each exercise is posed on this model with an average-sized adult (about 176 cm) and tested before it made the list.

Stations

How each exercise is checked

  1. Reach. Hands get to the handles, arm pads or hand-holds at every point of the movement.
  2. Resistance. The working part of the move pulls cable out or moves a lever the way the stack resists. If it doesn’t, the weight does nothing.
  3. Contact. Upper arms stay on the arm pad; the leg lever’s roller stays against the shin or calf.
  4. Cable path. The cable runs clear of your torso, head and legs.
  5. Space. No part of you passes through the tower, rail, column, seat or backrest.
  6. Joints. Elbows and knees stay within a normal range of bend.
